**Uniwax** is a turn-based hex-grid duel where a brave little white unicorn races a sneaky Shadow Unicorn across 12 hand-crafted islands.

**How to play:**<br/>
Each turn you move one hex, then the Shadow moves. Grab the Rainbow Gem, open the seal with a hidden key, or trap the Shadow in a dead-end corner — every level has a different goal, and the AI gets smarter, faster, and sneakier as you go. Collect ❤ hearts (your score) while dodging an opponent that hides in fog, steals your key, dashes, blocks your path, and fights dirty.

**Controls:**<br/>
     **Desktop:**<br/>
          Q/W/E/A/S/D to move, <br/>
          1–4 to use items, <br/>
          X/Space to wait, <br/>
          B undo, <br/>
          R retry, <br/>
          M/Esc menu.<br/><br/>
     **Mobile:**<br/>
          tap a highlighted hex to move, tap the bottom bar to use items, tap the cards to pick.<br/><br/>
Hints: <br/>
          find it a bit tricky in some tasks? Redo the previous ones!<br/>
